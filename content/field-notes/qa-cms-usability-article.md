## What a horizontal film does

A horizontal (16:9) film is built for screens people watch deliberately: the listing page, YouTube, an email campaign, a presentation to sellers. It has room for the full width of a room, the relationship between spaces and the setting around the house.

It suits properties where the story of the property depends on flow and scale: open plans, long sightlines, water, land.

::media[re-hamptons-beachfront]

## What a vertical reel does

A vertical (9:16) reel is made for the phone feed. It is shorter, faster and framed for one subject at a time: the entry, the kitchen island, the view from the primary suite.

It is the better choice when the launch is led by social channels, and it is included in the Social Media package for that reason.

::media[re-east-end-listing-reel]

## Why we shoot both formats on purpose

Cropping a horizontal film into a vertical one loses the edges of every frame, and a vertical shot stretched to horizontal looks like what it is. When both formats are needed, we plan and capture each one deliberately on the same visit.

## Which packages include which format

- Listing Starter: photography, drone and floor plan, no video.
- Social Media: vertical cinematic reel (with photography and drone).
- Luxury Media and Signature: both the horizontal film and the vertical reel.
- On their own: a single cinematic video or reel, or both from the same shoot, priced by square footage.

> Prices change with property size. The pricing page shows the exact amount for your square footage.

## How to decide

- Where will buyers and sellers actually see it? Listing page and presentations point to horizontal; a social-first launch points to vertical.
- Is the property's appeal in its scale and setting, or in a few standout moments? Scale favors horizontal; moments suit a reel.
- Will you also use the listing to market yourself? Both formats give you more to share after the sale.
