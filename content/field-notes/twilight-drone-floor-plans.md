## Drone: context a ground photo cannot give

Aerials show what a ground photo cannot: the water, the land, the neighborhood and how the home sits in it. On the East End that is often a large part of what a buyer is paying for.

It adds less when the lot is tight, the surroundings do not help the story, or local rules and weather limit the flight.

::media[ph-hampton-aerial-pool-beach]

## Twilight: the evening version of the home

Twilight stills photograph the home at dusk with interior and landscape lighting on. They carry the presentation into the evening and can lead a listing launch.

They suit homes with strong architecture, pools, terraces and designed lighting. A home with little exterior lighting or a plain facade gains less.

::media[ph-oceanfront-twilight-pool]

## Floor plan: understanding the layout before the visit

A schematic floor plan lets buyers understand the layout before they visit, so the people who book showings arrive better prepared. It is useful for most listings, and especially for larger or unusual layouts that photos alone cannot explain.
