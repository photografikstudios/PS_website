## The whole house

- Clean and organize every room that will be photographed.
- Clear countertops and other flat surfaces.
- Put garbage and recycling bins out of sight.
- If a twilight shoot is scheduled, check that every interior and exterior light works.

## Outside the house

- Move cars out of the driveway and away from the front of the house.
- Clear debris from the driveway and yard, and sweep the paths.
- Clear visible clutter from the roof and gutters.
- Put away bins, hoses and tools.
- Clean the barbecue, or store it.
- Wash windows and glass doors.
- Clean and straighten outdoor furniture; set out cushions and open umbrellas.
- Remove seasonal decorations.

## Light, windows and blinds

Interior photos are made with natural window light. For daytime interiors, keep artificial lights off, because mixing lamp light with daylight creates color casts.

Open horizontal and vertical blinds and shutters so the rooms fill with light.

Twilight is different: the lights are part of the picture, so every interior, exterior and landscape light needs to be working and switched on.

## Every interior room

- Put away personal items and family photos.
- Turn off ceiling fans.
- Clean mirrors and floors.
- Hide wastebaskets and pet gear.
- Switch off TVs and screens; hide cables and remotes.
- Put away children's toys.
- Remove extra furniture and decor, and small rugs that hide the floor.

## Kitchen

- Clear dishes, food, small appliances, soap and drying racks from the counters.
- Clean the appliances and the floor.
- Tidy open shelving.
- Remove magnets and photos from the refrigerator.

## Living spaces and bedrooms

- Organize bookshelves and reduce personal items.
- Remove furniture the room does not need.
- Make beds smoothly and arrange the pillows.
- Put away clothing and laundry.
- Clear bedside tables and anything stored under the beds.

## Bathrooms

- Close toilet lids.
- Remove toiletries, bath products, wastebaskets and cleaning tools.
- Remove extra towels and everything from the counters.

## Pool and spa

- Clean the water and the pool floor.
- Remove covers and pool equipment.
- Straighten chairs and umbrellas.
- For twilight, switch on pool lights and water features that will be photographed.
